The product

A bid-discovery workflow, not another giant procurement database.

The first version does one job: convert public federal opportunity data into a short, usable queue for cleaning contractors.

01

Collect

Pull recent opportunities from the official SAM.gov v2 API across janitorial and related building-service NAICS codes.

02

Rank

Apply deterministic scoring based on NAICS, service language, notice stage, set-aside, and response time.

03

Work

Filter, export, track Watch–Qualify–Bid stages, keep notes, and open the official notice from one screen.

04

Alert

Send a weekday digest matched to the subscriber’s states, minimum score, and certifications.
