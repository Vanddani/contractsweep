# Policy Review Sources

Official materials used to structure the ContractSweep policy update:

- Restore Online Shoppers' Confidence Act, 15 U.S.C. 8401–8405: clear material terms, express informed consent, and a simple mechanism to stop recurring charges.
- Minnesota Statutes 325G.56–325G.62: automatic-renewal definitions, offer terms, confirmation, and online termination-election requirements for covered consumer agreements.
- Minnesota Consumer Data Privacy Act guidance from the Minnesota Attorney General: privacy notice categories, purposes, rights process, contact method, retention disclosure, and update date.
- ContractSweep build report and legal/security checklist: seller identity, support information, subscription terms, refund/cancellation terms, government-affiliation disclaimer, score disclaimer, backup, and billing reconciliation.

The product is positioned for business use. Applicability of consumer-specific statutes depends on facts and should be reviewed by counsel.
