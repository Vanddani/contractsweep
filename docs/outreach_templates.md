# Outreach and Sales Templates

Use these as starting points. Personalize them and comply with applicable marketing, privacy, and anti-spam rules. Do not use purchased bulk lists or misleading sender identities.

## 15-minute discovery call

1. How do you currently find public or institutional cleaning opportunities?
2. Who checks the sources, and how often?
3. What immediately disqualifies a notice?
4. Which states or travel radius matter?
5. Which certifications or set-asides can you use?
6. How many plausible bids did you review in the last 90 days?
7. What information takes the longest to extract?
8. What happens when a viable notice is discovered late?
9. Show three live matches.
10. Ask: “Is this useful enough to pay $79 today and test for one month?”

Do not end with vague enthusiasm. Obtain a payment, a concrete objection, or a clear non-fit.

## Onboarding email

**Subject:** Your ContractSweep access and first alert settings

Your access is active.

1. Open the passwordless sign-in link.
2. Set your service states and eligible set-aside codes under Alert Profile.
3. Review the top five matches.
4. Move each to Watch, Qualify, Bid, or Dismissed.
5. Reply with the first result that should not have appeared and the first missing field that affected your decision.

The relevance score is not a win prediction. Read the official notice and attachments before acting.

## Cancellation interview

- What job were you hiring the product to do?
- Did it produce enough plausible opportunities?
- Were matches in the wrong geography or category?
- Was the information available elsewhere with little effort?
- Did your company lack time, eligibility, or appetite to bid?
- What would have made another month worthwhile?
- May the anonymous reason be included in product research?
